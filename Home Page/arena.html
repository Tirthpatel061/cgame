Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior coding experience?

No! CodeWarrior Arena is designed for complete beginners. We start from the very basics and guide you step by step.

How long does it take to complete all levels?

It depends on your pace! Beginners typically complete the journey in 3-6 months with regular practice (1-2 hours daily).

Can I practice at my own pace?

Absolutely! There are no time limits or deadlines. Learn whenever and wherever you want, at your own speed.

Is CodeWarrior Arena free?

Yes, basic access is free! Premium features with advanced challenges and personalized coaching are available for subscribers.

How do I get help when I'm stuck?

You can use the built-in hints, check solution walkthroughs, ask in the community forum, or use the AI assistant for help.
